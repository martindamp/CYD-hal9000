CYD Fire Eye — Web installer

A slowly pulsing eye of fire with a devilish pupil that follows your finger, for the Cheap Yellow Display. Install it straight from the browser — no Arduino IDE or PlatformIO needed.

Your browser does not support Web Serial. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (not mobile).
How to
  1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer.
  2. Plug the CYD into USB.
  3. Click Install CYD Fire Eye and pick the serial port (CH340/CP2102).
  4. Done — no Wi-Fi needed. Skip the Wi-Fi step if the installer offers it.

On the device: tap to make the eye look at you and flare up, hold to make the pupil grow and the fire roar.
